Privacy & Terms

Vani.ai is a final-year capstone project. This page describes what the software actually does with your data.

Privacy Policy

Live audio (host)

When the host presses Start, microphone audio is streamed from the host's browser to the Vani.ai server and on to Speechmatics for speech-to-text. Vani.ai does not record or store the audio.

Captions

The text produced from the audio is sent to the host and to everyone who joined the same invite code. To reach viewers on different servers, captions pass through a Redis message channel (Upstash). They are delivered and not stored there.

Translation

If a viewer turns on translation, each finished sentence is sent from the viewer's browser to Google Translate.

AI summary and Vani Bot

When the host presses Generate Summary, or a viewer asks Vani Bot a question, the transcript so far (and the question) is sent to Google Gemini to produce the answer.

Saved transcripts, settings and profile

Save, History, Settings and Profile are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). There are no accounts. Clearing your browser data removes them, and they are not visible to anyone else.

Contact form

Messages sent through the contact form (name, email and message) are stored so the team can reply. Only the latest 500 messages are kept.

Offline (edge) mode

The offline fallback downloads a Whisper speech model to your browser and transcribes on your own device. In that mode, audio does not leave your device.

Terms of Service

  • Vani.ai is provided as-is for demonstration and educational use, without warranty of any kind.
  • Captions, translations and summaries are generated automatically and can be wrong. Don't rely on them for medical, legal or safety-critical decisions.
  • Only stream audio you have the right to share, and tell the people being recorded that their speech is being transcribed.
  • Don't use the service for confidential conversations. Audio and text are processed by the third-party services listed above.
  • Don't misuse the service, for example by flooding rooms, the contact form or the AI endpoints with automated requests.
